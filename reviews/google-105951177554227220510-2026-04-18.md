Lovely breakfast and cappuccinos here! Good food whether you go healthy yogurt bowl or luscious bacon and egg sandwich. Luke and Katie, the friendly owners, gave us tips on rainy-day sights in the area. Don’t be fooled by the old food truck photo on maps — they’re now in a nice cafe in the same building as the local swim-and-gym community center. And they open at 8! Very happy, a great spot.
