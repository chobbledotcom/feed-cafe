Absolutely love this place! Been numerous times! The breakfast bagels are banging! Love burger nights too, such a huge choice to choose from, something to suit everyone's tastes! Really friendly staff, fun atmosphere, you can take your own booze to sit in the little garden at the side(only a handful benches however) The food is top quality, tastes so delicious and cooked to order. Easy to make amendments aswell with no fuss. Highly recommend visiting here if your in Hayle, best place around for coffee and food. Excellent value for money. Can't wait to go again as their is a good variety of healthy food options such as super food salads aswell naughty but nice treats! Soft drinks and cakes are also sold here!
