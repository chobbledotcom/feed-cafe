Food here is absolutely phenomenal! Coffee is great.<br>I eat here a few times a week and have tried almost everything on the menu and can't fault a thing. Party food for my daughters birthday was a massive hit! The kids especially loved the mini burgers and smoothies. Lush outdoor seating area, really child friendly with little watering cans to water the flowers. I would definitely recommend to anyone that will listen to me go on and on about amazing food!
