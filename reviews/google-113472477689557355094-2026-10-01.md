One of the best value places to eat in St Ives. I've been coming here a while now and I'm always impressed with the quality and size of the portions I get for the money I pay. The warm meals are always fresh and tasty, whilst the salads and sandwiches are always made up of nice, high quality stuff. The staff are all lovely too.
