My sister and I went into Feed on our way into St Ives via the Park and Ride . We tried it,  as the cafe across the road is now closed.<br>This turned out to be a blessing as the coffee and staff were excellent.<br>This is not your average leisure centre cafe .<br>It has an excellent selection , including some healthy options.<br>We were so impressed we went back there to have our lunch and were not disappointed.<br>I highly recommend it. I think it has only been there a short while. So give it a try.
