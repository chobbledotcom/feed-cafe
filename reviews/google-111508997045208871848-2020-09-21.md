The coffee is lovely! The chocolate orange / jaffa cake brownie was absolutely incredible (it had an actual jaffa cake inside). My auntie had a bakewell slice and was amazed by the fact it had fresh cherries in it! The bacon bap was tasty, the bacon could have been crispier but the bun was bouncy and yummy. A sweet seating area with cute fairy lights. My only complaint is that the bin needed emptying as it was on the verge of overflowing. Will likely go again soon!
